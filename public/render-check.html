TOPIC 7 - PROBABILITY

Key notes and worked examples

6/8
Demonstrate by drawing, painting or making in the topics below.
Probability scale
Impossible (0) - Unlikely - Even chance - Likely - Certain (1).
Probability as a fraction
P(event) = favourable outcomes / total equally likely outcomes.
Equally likely outcomes
Each outcome has the same chance: a fair coin, spinner or dice.
Complementary events
P(event) + P(not event) = 1.

Worked examples

Q: Bag: 3 red, 5 blue, 2 green balls. Find P(blue).
  1. Total = 3 + 5 + 2 = 10
  2. P(blue) = 5/10
Answer: 1/2
Q: P(winning) = 1/4. What is P(NOT winning)?
  1. P(not winning) = 1 - 1/4
Answer: 3/4
Think further

Why do some events feel more likely than the numbers say they are?
